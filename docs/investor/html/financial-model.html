How numbers are calculated
  • B2C subscription: Student Monthly $9.99/mo ≈ $120/yr per paying user (Yearly $89.99 is the same order of magnitude; model uses ~$120).
  • B2C cash (summary): year-end paying users × ~$120 — Y1 50 × $120 = $6,000 · Y2 150 × $120 = $18,000 · Y3 300 × $120 = $36,000.
  • B2C cash (detail): Month-by-month ramp uses $9.99 MRR plus a few $89.99 yearly prepays; totals still tie to $6,000 in Y1.
  • Campus = $10,000 pilot invoiced in month 9. Years 2–3 = Small $15K + Mid $25K licenses.
